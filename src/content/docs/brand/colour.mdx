## Contrast

Hues are pretty, but text still has to be read. These are the pairs that pass WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 for body text) on the website's two themes. Each hue gets the bright 400 step on ink and a deep step on paper. Amber needs 800 on paper, because 700 lands at 4.4:1 on a raised card, and "nearly" isn't a contrast ratio.

| Hue | Text on ink (dark) | Text on paper (light) | Paper on the light step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Emerald | 400, 9.6:1 | 700, 5.3:1 | 5.3:1 |
| Violet | 400, 6.8:1 | 700, 6.8:1 | 6.8:1 |
| Pink | 400, 7.0:1 | 700, 5.8:1 | 5.8:1 |
| Blue | 400, 7.2:1 | 700, 6.4:1 | 6.4:1 |
| Amber | 400, 11.0:1 | 800, 6.8:1 | 6.8:1 |
| Leaf | 400, 8.2:1 | 700, 5.8:1 | 5.8:1 |

Ink on a 500 block passes for every hue except violet, which comes in at 4.3:1. That's fine for shapes, icons and large text (3:1), so keep violet 500 for those and put body text on violet 400 or 700 instead.

## Shades in the mark

Each ring takes a fixed shade from its scale. The left half uses the first shade and the right half the next one down.

| Ring | Dark backgrounds | Slate on dark | Light backgrounds | Slate on light |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ring 1 | 300 / 400 | 200 / 300 | 500 / 600 | 400 / 500 |
| Ring 2 | 500 / 600 | 400 / 500 | 700 / 800 | 600 / 700 |
| Ring 3 | 400 / 500 | 300 / 400 | 600 / 700 | 500 / 600 |
| Ring 4 | 300 / 400 | 200 / 300 | 500 / 600 | 400 / 500 |
| Ring 5 | 500 / 600 | 400 / 500 | 700 / 800 | 600 / 700 |

The core is paper on dark backgrounds and ink on light ones.

## Colour around the mark

- Body text on ink is paper, and secondary text is slate 300.
- On ink, a template's 300 shade is its accent: kickers, command chips and highlights on its social preview.
- On a docs site, a template's 400 shade is the accent in dark mode and its 700 shade in light mode. The [Starlight themes](/brand/kit/tokens/) set this up for you.
